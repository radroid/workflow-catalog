### 3. Known limitations for P10-A
One line each, with an example and whether the validator is too strict (refuses honest text) or too loose (passes dishonest text). Priority: too-loose first, since dishonesty is the more expensive miss.

**Too loose (passes dishonest text):**
- Packet finding 1: a possessive role phrase states an uncited title. "Platform Engineer at Fernwood Labs, 2019–2021, and the platform team's manager [C9]." passes though C9 never states "manager." (r3#28, r3-realistic-reworded#28)
- Packet finding 2: a domain-like dotted word at a sentence's end is read as a multi-part abbreviation ("x.com." matches the same rule as "e.g."/"B.Eng.", by part count alone), so a fabricated sentence rides along. "Won the hackathon run by x.com. Shipped the on-call rotation tooling used by three engineering teams [C3]." passes. (r3#58)
- Packet finding 4: a capitalized listed abbreviation outside brackets ("Rd.", and "Inc." via the always-abbreviation list) never ends a sentence, whatever follows — a deliberate, documented trade (text.ts's own comment) that also keeps "Mt. Hood"/"Ft. Worth"/"Lt. Quill" working. "Worked from the office on Quill Rd. Shipped the on-call rotation tooling used by three engineering teams [C3]." passes. (r4#27, r5#80)
- Packet finding 7: seniority named outside a bracket immediately after the title isn't read as raising it (Z2's bracket-join only looks at a bracket adjacent to the found title). "Platform Engineer, Fernwood Labs (Staff), 2019–2021 [C9]."; "Platform Engineer — Staff — Fernwood Labs, 2019–2021 [C9]."; "Platform Engineer, Staff, Fernwood Labs, 2019–2021 [C9]."; "Platform Engineer (Staff level on the payments team) at Fernwood Labs, 2019–2021 [C9]." all pass. (r5#34, #36, #37, #38)
- Packet finding 7: "Then was engineering manager overseeing payments at Fernwood Labs from 2019 to 2021 [C9]." passes — no subject before "was" for the title-context rule to anchor on (the sibling case "Most recently was …" is correctly refused). (r5#70)
- Packet finding 7: a possessive before a role noun after "was" isn't read as a title. "I was Harbor's platform engineer from 2021 to 2023 [C10]." passes though C10 states "Engineering manager." (r5#72)
- Packet finding 8: an abbreviation inside a closing bracket only ends a sentence before a capital letter or a citation marker, never before a lower-case word (Z2's ruling, needed so "(Sr.) at Fernwood Labs" stays one sentence) — so a fabricated list before it rides along. "Won the hackathon, the demo day, the pitch night (etc.) shipped the on-call rotation tooling used by three engineering teams [C3]." passes. (r5#32)
- Packet finding 8: a possessive before "scores" always reads it as a noun (Z7), regardless of what follows "of," so it can't tell a legitimate metric from an inflated headcount. "Mentored Harbor's scores of new engineers joining its platform team [C14]." passes. (r5#88)
- F1: a claim's joined bracket that names *someone else's* role is read as the subject's own title. "Served as CTO at Fernwood Labs from 2019 to 2021 [C11]." passes, citing a claim whose bracket says "reporting to the CTO." (gate#31, and gate#32 against a claim with the same wording unbracketed)

**Too strict (refuses honest text):**
- Packet finding 5 (Y1's costs): a revision-3 present-tense word refuses even when it reads as an ordinary verb, not an open-ended claim. "Redesigned the ledger service so Northwind Labs' balances remain consistent [C1]." and "Redesigned the ledger service behind what is today Northwind Labs' billing [C1]." are refused. (r4#23, r4#24)

"Lead developer experience work for three engineering teams through the on-call rotation tooling [C3]."; "Senior engineer feedback shaped the on-call rotation tooling used by three engineering teams [C3]."; "Staff engineer reviews shaped the on-call rotation tooling used by three engineering teams [C3]."; "Head engineering hiring for the payments infrastructure team at Northwind Labs [C1]." are all refused. - Packet finding 8: a claim's own bracket, cited without it, is refused — Z2 compares titles as one whole string including the bracket, so honestly omitting your own seniority bracket reads the same as adding one that isn't there. "Platform Engineer at Fernwood Labs, 2019–2021 [C13]." is refused though C13 states "Platform Engineer (Senior) at Fernwood Labs, 2019–2021." (r5#29)

- F2 (a cost of fixing F1): "Platform Engineer (Senior, reporting to the CTO) at Fernwood Labs, 2019–2021 [C12]." is refused ("cto") though C12 has no such bracket — the per-part title matcher can't yet tell "a bracket naming someone else's role" from "a bracket that's part of the subject's own title." (gate#33, and gate#34 for the same bracket content outside the join)
- F3: an adjective before "scores" no longer makes it a noun. "Kept perfect scores of 100 on the Ledgerkit documentation's Lighthouse accessibility audits [C13]." is refused as the fabricated quantity "scores of." Not a simple word-list fix: the same adjective ("top") must stay a *count* in "Mentored top scores of engineers joining the Harbor platform team [C14]." - F5: "I am the engineer on call for the payments infrastructure team at Northwind Labs [C1]." is refused ("engineer") — the same recorded cost as "I was the engineer on call," which the title-context rule can't tell from a genuine title claim. (gate#20, gate#21, r5#66)
